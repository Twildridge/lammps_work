BACKGROUND
The wide random 10-bead gel's compression (Expanse job 55745206, 7,600 SU) was wasted: the plates seat on
the OUTERMOST polymer bead, that gel has 13-16 sigma of surface fringe (L_bb - L_rg; attached loops, the
gel itself is equilibrated), and the 9 sigma plate closure at strain 0.10 went into the fringe. The Rg
thickness fell 2.5 % for a nominal 10 % and the stress (0.0045 +/- 0.0007) never levelled.

WHAT CHANGED
1. simulations/triaxial_compression_two_pist/triaxial_compression_two_pist.lmp
   - seat_mode (index, default bb; SEAT_MODE): bb = outermost bead as before; rg = the Rg face
     (COM +/- L_rg/2, the half-density face), so the plates fold the fringe onto the dense gel. The strain
     bookkeeping is the bounding box in both modes (L0_bb of the seated gel, closure = strain x L0_bb).
   - three guards, each prints ">>> GUARD ..." and does `quit 1`; 0 turns a guard off:
       fringe_max    (default 1.0)  after Phase 0, seat_mode bb only: fringe > fringe_max x planned closure
       prestrain_max (default 0.10) after the seated settle: Rg thickness seated vs free
       rg_strain_min (default 0.5)  end of every hold: Rg strain / applied strain
   - new log lines: ">>> fringe check: ...", ">>> seating faces (seat_mode ..., window means): ...",
     ">>> reference check (seat_mode ...): ... pre-strain ...", ">>> strain check: ...".
2. simulations/compress_slab/compress_slab.lmp
   - seat_mode already existed (bb default, rg). Added the same three guards (per-axis fringe, volumetric
     Rg pre-strain, Rg volume strain / applied strain) and the same check lines, plus an early seat_mode
     validation.
3. scripts/lib/run_common.sh: KNOBS now forwards fringe_max, rg_strain_min, prestrain_max (seat_mode and
   unload_margin were already there).
4. docs/running_simulations.md: paragraph "SEAT_MODE and the credit guards".
Shear and permeation decks were NOT changed: the periodic shear drives polymer layers defined from the Rg
faces, the isolated shear seats on the Rg face by default, and the permeation deck has no load plates.

Things I should know before production (tell me if the smoke runs contradict any of this):
  - With the defaults, a gel whose fringe exceeds the planned closure is now stopped in bb mode about
    2 minutes in. That includes the wide random 10-bead slab (ratio about 1.7) and the 12-cell cube in
    compress_slab with bb seating (ratio about 2 at eps_vol 0.10, about 4 at 0.05), so the standard
    compress_slab batches need SEAT_MODE=rg for that cube. Past data agree: the bb cube sweep of
    2026-10-19 gave Rg volume strains of 4-14 % of the applied strain, the rg run of 2026-10-11 gave 77 %.
  - In rg mode the reference is the gel in contact with the plates, so it carries a preload and a
    pre-strain. Both are printed, and the analysis takes moduli as increments from that reference.
  - In rg mode with the unload, the plates must retract beyond the seated gap (UNLOAD_MARGIN, about half
    the fringe) or the re-swelling gel meets them again.
